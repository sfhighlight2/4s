Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains how The Aesthetic Success (“we”, “us”), which presents the 4S Summit, collects, uses and shares information when you visit this website, register for an event or buy a replay. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you reserve a seat or buy a replay we ask for your first and last name, email address, phone number, practice or business name, and, for event registrations, your practice's annual revenue range. Our checkout also collects the details needed to process your payment.

Payment information. Payments are processed by our payment providers (GoHighLevel and Stripe). We do not store your full card number on this website.

Information collected automatically. When you browse the site we and our service providers collect technical and usage information, including your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the page that referred you, and campaign parameters in the link you followed (such as utm_source or fbclid). We use:

  • Meta Pixel, to measure the performance of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, including page views, registrations and purchases.
  • Hotjar, to understand how visitors use the site through heatmaps and session recordings.
  • Your browser's local storage, to remember whether you have already seen our pop-up and to pass your order details from checkout to the confirmation page so we can record the purchase.

How we use your information

  • To process your registration or purchase and deliver your ticket, replay access and event information.
  • To contact you about your registration, the event, travel and hotel details, and related programs, by email, phone call or text message.
  • To understand which practices attend so we can tailor event content and follow-up.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and our website.
  • To comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your registration and the 4S Summit. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and ask us to stop calling by contacting us at the address below. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the event and this website, under agreements that limit their use of it:

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale or reorganization of our business.

Your choices

  • Marketing email: use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Text messages: reply STOP.
  • Advertising: you can adjust ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings, and block cookies or tracking in your browser.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to records we must keep by law (for example, payment records). Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under local privacy law.

Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our events and services, maintain business and tax records, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Children

This site and our events are intended for business professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
